# Personas

Drawn from `requirements.md` (intent analysis and FR1 to FR8), `business-overview.md` (users and roles) and the answers in `user-stories-questions.md` (Q2, Q5). All names are synthetic.

## P1 Jordan Rivera, pharmacist in charge, Store 1

- **Role:** pharmacist; account `j.rivera@elmstreetrx.example`.
- **Goals:** respond to a recall fast and precisely; verify fills; decide on flagged prescriptions and stock suggestions.
- **Pain points:** recalls today mean warning everyone or no one; stock decisions are guesswork; two stores make it worse.
- **Tech comfort:** medium. **Frequency:** all day, every day.
- **May do:** everything in Store 1, including transfers, scenarios, demo reset and acting on stock suggestions (Q5).

## P2 Kiran Patel, technician, Store 1

- **Role:** technician; account `k.patel@elmstreetrx.example`.
- **Goals:** check in deliveries quickly and correctly; fill prescriptions with the right package.
- **Pain points:** suspect or damaged packages slip through; serials are typed by hand.
- **Tech comfort:** medium. **Frequency:** all day.
- **May do:** receive, shelve and fill; see stock suggestions but not act on them (Q5).

## P3 Maya Diaz, cashier, Store 1

- **Role:** cashier; account `m.diaz@elmstreetrx.example`.
- **Goals:** ring up a customer's over-the-counter items and prescriptions in one sale, without seeing clinical details.
- **Pain points:** two separate counters and systems for shopping and pickup.
- **Tech comfort:** low to medium. **Frequency:** all day.
- **May do:** register sales and prescription pickup only (Q5).

## P4 Sam Okoro, pharmacist, Store 2

- **Role:** pharmacist at the second store; a new synthetic demo account.
- **Goals:** run Store 2 without seeing Store 1's patients; receive stock moved from Store 1.
- **Pain points:** a recall in one store must not be missed in the other.
- **Tech comfort:** medium. **Frequency:** all day.
- **May do:** everything in Store 2 only (Q2, Q4).

## P5 The judge

- **Role:** a Kiroween judge with about 5 minutes to start the pilot and 3 to understand it.
- **Goals:** run it, see the recall story, check the claims (accuracy, timing, lot capture) and see how Kiro was used.
- **Pain points:** setup steps, unclear claims, apps that need real accounts.
- **Tech comfort:** high. **Frequency:** once.
- **May do:** sign in with the demo accounts the README lists (Q2, Q5).

## Priority

P1 and P5 carry the demo story; P3 shows the "everything in one place" pitch; P2 supports the morning delivery scene; P4 shows the two stores (Q1, Q2).
